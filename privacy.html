Legal · v1 draft

Privacy, plainly.

Effective August 30, 2026 · Counsel review required before public launch.

What this covers

HMD Storefront is building a local-first storefront and loyalty platform for independent shops. This draft describes this marketing site and, at a high level, information processed by the storefront app.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the service, this may include your name, email address, phone number, shop details, loyalty activity, and consent choices. This site uses only essential local storage for your cookie-consent choice at this time.

How information is used

We use information to provide and improve the service, support shops and customers, maintain security, and honor documented consent choices. We do not claim that the service is compliant with any particular law; the product and this policy are being developed with transparency in mind.

Loyalty-program disclosures

Points, check-ins, rewards, eligibility, expiration, and cross-shop redemption rules are set out in the app and by participating shops. Earning points is not, by itself, permission to use information for unrelated purposes. Network or system rewards will be labeled separately from shop-specific rewards. No points balances, member-shop counts, or redemption totals are claimed on this marketing site.

Do Not Sell or Share / opt out

V1 draft: HMD Storefront does not sell personal information from this marketing site. If a future product feature involves sharing information for a benefit, the applicable notice will describe it and provide an opt-out where required. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or opting out of a disclosed data use, contact privacy@themeansquares.com. Counsel should review these definitions and processes before launch.

Consent and choices

Customers should be able to understand purposes of data use, opt in where required, and revoke consent. The exact revenue-share and data-use mechanics are disclosed in the product and applicable shop notices.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@themeansquares.com.
