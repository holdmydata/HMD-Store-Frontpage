What this covers

Mean Squares is building a local-first storefront and loyalty platform for independent shops. This policy describes information collected on this marketing site and, at a high level, the information processed by the storefront app.

Information we may collect

When you contact us or use the app, this may include your name, email address, phone number, shop details, loyalty activity, and consent choices. This site uses only essential local storage for your cookie-consent choice at this time.

How information is used

We use information to provide and improve the service, support shops and customers, maintain security, and honor documented consent choices. We do not claim that the service is compliant with any particular law; the product and this policy are being developed with transparency in mind.

Consent and choices

Customers should be able to understand the purposes of data use, opt in where required, and revoke consent. The exact revenue-share and data-use mechanics are disclosed in the product and applicable shop notices. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@themeansquares.com. This draft is not legal advice.
