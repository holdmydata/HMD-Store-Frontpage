A little more together

One login.
More places to belong.

A plain-language look at the planned cross-shop rewards network. Availability and terms may vary by participating shop.

01

Earn where you already go.

Use one HMD Storefront account to check in and earn points at participating member shops. Each shop chooses its own eligible visits, rewards, and terms.

02

Use points across the network.

When a reward is marked eligible for network redemption, you can use points from your account at another participating shop. The app shows the balance, cost, and conditions before you redeem.

03

System rewards fill the gaps.

Some rewards may come from the HMD Storefront program itself rather than one shop. They will be labeled clearly, with their eligibility and expiration details shown up front.

The promise

No mystery math.

Your points balance, the shop offering a reward, and the information you agreed to share should be visible in one place. Consent is separate from earning points, and you can change your choices through the app.
