About the service

Mean Squares provides tools for independent shops to manage customer relationships, loyalty rewards, consent records, and related storefront workflows. Features and availability may change as the service develops.

Accounts and responsible use

Keep account details accurate and secure. Use the service lawfully, respect customer choices, and do not attempt to access another person’s account or data. Shop operators are responsible for the notices and rewards they configure.

Points and rewards

Each shop controls its published reward rules, including eligibility, availability, expiration, and redemption limits. Participating shops may offer rewards usable across the network; system rewards will be identified separately. Points have no cash value unless the applicable published terms say otherwise. We make no claim about future reward availability or network size.

Customer information

Use customer information only for disclosed, permitted purposes. Do not treat a loyalty reward as permission to collect or use information beyond the customer’s stated choices. See our Privacy Policy for the current draft.

Opt-out and disclosures

V1 draft: any sale or sharing of personal information, if introduced, will be described in an applicable notice with an opt-out where required. Counsel must review the program disclosures and operational process before public launch.

Changes and availability

We may update these terms as the product and legal review progress. We will publish the current version on this page. The service may be unavailable for maintenance or changes.
